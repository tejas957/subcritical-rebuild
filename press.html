Subcritical Systems Secures Clear Regulatory Path with U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission

Jan 12, 2026

Subcritical Systems achieved a major milestone by confirming with the NRC that its Energy Amplifier technology can be licensed under existing regulatory frameworks (10 CFR Part 70 and Part 30), eliminating the need for new rulemaking and significantly accelerating the path to commercialization. This regulatory clarity de-risks the timeline to market and validates the company's approach with federal authorities. The announcement follows productive engagement with NRC staff and legal guidance from former NRC Commissioner Jeff Merrifield, positioning Subcritical Systems to lead U.S. deployment of accelerator-driven subcritical nuclear energy systems while similar projects advance in Europe and China.

Subcritical Systems CTO Stuart Henderson to Present at The Advanced Reactors Summit XIII & Technology Trailblazers Showcase

Feb 10–12, 2026 · Seattle, WA

Stuart Henderson, Chief Technology Officer at Subcritical Systems, leads technology development for accelerator-driven, subcritical nuclear energy systems and the enabling superconducting radiofrequency (SRF) and accelerator technologies required to deliver them at scale. He previously served as Director of the U.S. Department of Energy's Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility (Jefferson Lab), and held senior roles at Argonne National Laboratory, Fermilab, and Oak Ridge National Laboratory's Spallation Neutron Source. He is a Fellow of the American Physical Society.
